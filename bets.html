Week – Lines

BoardFair lines from the Contendathon's 20,000 simulated weeks, with no house cut. Pick a price to post a bet, and anyone in the league can take the other side. Every game locks at the week's first NFL kickoff, and bets settle on Sleeper's final scores.
  • Moneyline: pick the winner. −150 means risk $150 to win $100; +150 means risk $100 to win $150. The person who takes the other side risks exactly what you'd win.
  • Spread and total: even money, so both sides risk the same. A spread or total that lands exactly on the number is a push, and both stakes go back.
  • Stakes are $1–$100. You can cancel a bet until someone takes it; untaken bets expire at lock.
  • This is a scoreboard of who owes whom. No money moves through the site, so settle up however your group does.

Team propsBack a team to finish the week with the most points in the league, or the fewest. Odds come from how often it happens in the 20,000 simulated weeks. Whoever takes your bet has "not them" at the mirrored price. A tie for the top (or bottom) is a push.

Top scorer by positionBack a player to be the week's top scorer at his position, among every rostered Kooks player projected to play (bench included). Only players with at least a 1% chance are offered, but everyone in the pool counts. Whoever takes your bet has "not them" at the mirrored price. A tie for the top is a push, and so is a player who's ruled out before lock.

Open betsBets people have posted that nobody has taken yet. Taking one locks it in: you risk what the poster would win, to win their stake. You can cancel your own until someone takes it.

In progressEvery bet that's been taken and is waiting on the games, for everyone in the league. Each side risks what the other would win, and the winner takes both. They settle once the week's scores are final.

My betsEvery bet you've posted or taken, with its status. Settled bets show what you won or lost.

StandingsEveryone's record and net winnings on settled bets. At risk now is the money in matched bets that haven't settled yet. Names come from each person's Google account.
